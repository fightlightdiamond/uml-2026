How the engine resolves one hit

The five-tier pipeline of SR_GameServer. Every rate enters the formula as a decimal. Physical and magic streams run in parallel, armor is subtracted first, and balance is applied after that.

The working formula

Each hit resolves two independent streams \(t \in \{\mathrm{phy}, \mathrm{mag}\}\). A successful block stops the hit and the total is 0. An inactive stream contributes 0. An active stream that fails to penetrate armor is forced to 1 before a critical is considered.

\[ \mathrm{Raw}_{t}=\frac{(A_{t}+B_{t})\,(1+C_{t})}{1+D_{t}}-E_{t} \]
\[ \mathrm{Calc}_{t}=\mathrm{Raw}_{t}\times F_{t}\times(1+G_{t})\times H_{t}\times L \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \mathrm{DMG}_{t}&=0 && \text{off}\\ \mathrm{DMG}_{t}&=1 && \text{on, Raw}\le 0\\ \mathrm{DMG}_{t}&=\max\!\big(1,\lfloor\mathrm{Calc}_{t}\rfloor\big) && \text{on, Raw}>0 \end{aligned} \]
\[ S=(1+c)\,\mathrm{DMG}_{\mathrm{phy}}+\mathrm{DMG}_{\mathrm{mag}} \]
\[ \mathrm{Total}= \begin{cases} 0 & \text{block}\\ S\cdot Z & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} \]

\(c = 1\) on a critical hit and \(c = 0\) on a normal hit. A critical turns \((1+c)\) into 2 on the physical stream only. \(Z\) is the Berserk factor on the output total: \(Z = 1\) with no Berserk, and original servers usually use \(Z = 2\). \(F\) here is already a decimal and already capped at 1.20.

Two places that are easy to get wrong

The two rules below were checked against the SR_GameServer disassembly and against measurements taken in game. The rest of this page uses those rules.

Balance Rate comes after armor

\(F_{\mathrm{phy}}\) and \(F_{\mathrm{mag}}\) are multiplied in the amplification step, after defense has been subtracted. The attack stat on the character sheet does not already include this factor. The value that enters the product is a decimal, capped at 1.20. A UI reading of 107.788% becomes \(F = 1.07788\). The STR and INT formulas in Tier 1 already return that capped decimal, so the result is not divided by 100 again.

A summary that spread online wrote \(F\) as a percent from 0 to 120, without dividing by 100 and without the 120% cap. On Test 1, multiplying by 107.788 instead of 1.07788 turns \(\mathrm{Calc}\) into 1,057,322.34, one hundred times the pipeline value of 10,573.22.

Absorption uses diminishing division

\(D\) is the sum of same-element absorption on the four jewelry pieces, converted to a decimal. That sum routinely exceeds 100%. The Wizard set in Test 1 is \(23.5\% + 23.7\% + 30.5\% + 26.2\% = 103.9\%\), so \(D = 1.039\). The engine divides by \(1+D\). At \(D = 1.039\), the attack remaining before armor is \(1/2.039 \approx 49.04\%\).

The subtraction \((1-D)\) on that same set gives \(1 - 1.039 = -0.039\), which pulls the numerator 11,971.84 negative before the 1,380 armor is even subtracted. The correct pipeline still produces a positive Raw of 4,491.43.

One hit, in order

  1. 1Build the stats as decimalsSTR, INT, the milestone \(M\), \(F\) already capped at 1.20, the attack bounds, \(D\), and \(E\). Ignore Monster Defense in PvE sets \(E = 0\).
  2. 2Ask the shieldIf the target holds a shield and Blocking Ratio succeeds, Total = 0 and the hit stops.
  3. 3Roll A and BDraw a value in [Min, Max]. Attack Rating and Parry only pull the roll toward Max or Min. Silkroad has no dodge.
  4. 4Raw, per stream\((A+B)(1+C)/(1+D) - E\). Physical and magic are computed separately.
  5. 5Multiply by F, G, H, L\(F\) is a decimal, at most 1.20, and it stands after the armor subtraction.
  6. 6Apply the floorStream off → 0. Stream on and Raw ≤ 0 → 1. Stream on and Raw > 0 → \(\max(1, \lfloor \mathrm{Calc}\rfloor)\).
  7. 7Add, then criticalA normal hit adds the two DMG values. A critical doubles the floored physical DMG, then adds magic DMG.
  8. 8Berserk and multi-hitEach hit runs again from the shield check through critical and produces its own \(S\). \(Z\) multiplies each \(S\), or multiplies the sum of the \(S\) values once: both give the same number because \(Z\) does not change per hit. A blocked hit stays 0.

One hit after the roll

Physical stream

Raw from physical A, B, C, D, E. Multiply by \(F_{\mathrm{phy}}\), \(1+G\), \(H\), \(L\). An active stream has a minimum of 1, and only then does a critical double that integer.

Magic stream

The same steps, with the magic variables. The imbue portion uses that portion’s own coefficient; on a weapon hit, that portion usually sits on the magic stream. A critical does not multiply this stream.

Add the two DMG values → a critical doubles only the physical cell → Berserk multiplies the total. A block never enters this diagram.

Tier 1 — Base stats and the character sheet

Every input is a decimal before it leaves this tier. For example, 99% is 0.99, 103.9% is 1.039, 208% is 2.08, and a balance of 107.788% is 1.07788.

STR and INT

Each level, the character gains 1 STR, 1 INT, and 3 points to allocate. The allocatable pool at the current level is \(3(\mathrm{Lv}-1)\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \mathrm{STR}_{\mathrm{total}}&=\mathrm{BaseSTR}+(\mathrm{Lv}-1)\\ &\quad+\mathrm{AllocSTR}+\mathrm{GearBuffSTR} \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \mathrm{INT}_{\mathrm{total}}&=\mathrm{BaseINT}+(\mathrm{Lv}-1)\\ &\quad+\mathrm{AllocINT}+\mathrm{GearBuffINT} \end{aligned} \]

BaseSTR and BaseINT come from race and class on the character sheet. This page does not embed a base-stat table. The GearBuff field is STR or INT from gear and buffs, entered as one number.

Balance factor

The level milestone:

\[ M = 28 + 4 \times \mathrm{Lv} \]

Physical balance, capped at 1.20. The factor \(\tfrac{2}{3}\) makes STR = 0 return exactly \(\tfrac{1}{3}\). A real character always has BaseSTR and the STR granted by levels, so with no allocated STR, \(F_{\mathrm{phy}}\) still sits above 33.3%.

\[ F_{\mathrm{phy}}=\min\!\left(1.20,\; 1-\frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{M-\mathrm{STR}_{\mathrm{total}}}{M}\right) \]

The same quantity, written as the ratio of STR to the milestone:

\[ F_{\mathrm{phy}}=\min\!\left(1.20,\; \frac{1}{3}+\frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{\mathrm{STR}_{\mathrm{total}}}{M}\right) \]

The 1.20 cap is reached when \(\mathrm{STR}_{\mathrm{total}} \ge 1.3\,M\). From that point on, more STR does not raise \(F_{\mathrm{phy}}\).

Magic balance has no \(\tfrac{1}{3}\)-style floor. Full STR leaves INT low, so \(F_{\mathrm{mag}}\) is low with it. The cap is also 1.20, reached when \(\mathrm{INT}_{\mathrm{total}} \ge 1.2\,M\).

\[ F_{\mathrm{mag}}=\min\!\left(1.20,\; \frac{\mathrm{INT}_{\mathrm{total}}}{M}\right) \]

Base attack, before Mastery

Each formula is run twice, once for Min and once for Max, because the reinforce percents on the two ends can differ. Reinforce is entered in percent points: an 80% line is divided by 100.

\[ \begin{aligned} \mathrm{PhyAtk}&=\mathrm{WepPhy}+\mathrm{Flat}_{\mathrm{phy}}\\ &\quad+\mathrm{STR}_{\mathrm{total}}\times\frac{\mathrm{Reinforce}}{100} \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \mathrm{MagAtk}&=\mathrm{WepMag}+\mathrm{Flat}_{\mathrm{mag}}\\ &\quad+\mathrm{INT}_{\mathrm{total}}\times\frac{\mathrm{Reinforce}}{100} \end{aligned} \]

Do not double-count Mastery. If \(A\) is weapon attack before Mastery, put Mastery in \(C\). If \(A\) is the character-sheet number that already includes Mastery, leave Mastery out of \(C\). One source goes into only one of the two places.

Defense and absorption

\(E_{\mathrm{phy}}\) and \(E_{\mathrm{mag}}\) are same-element defense after armor, armor reinforce by STR or INT, defense buffs, and defense debuffs such as Division or Weaken. Ignore Monster Defense works in PvE only: when that line is active, set \(E_{t} = 0\).

\(D_{\mathrm{phy}}\) and \(D_{\mathrm{mag}}\) are the summed same-element absorption percents of two rings, the earring, and the necklace, divided by 100. \(D\) is not capped at 1. Four pieces at 103.9% give \(D = 1.039\), and the pipeline denominator is \(1 + D = 2.039\).

Tier 2 — Block and the Min–Max roll

Silkroad Online has no dodge. Every hit passes two gates before it becomes damage.

Block gate: the target is holding a shield and the roll hits Blocking Ratio. That hit’s total damage is 0. Crit, Berserk, and both streams are not applied to a blocked hit. A multi-hit skill asks Block once per hit; a blocked hit is 0, and the other hits still run.

Roll gate: the server draws \(A_{t}\) and \(B_{t}\) inside \([\mathrm{Min}, \mathrm{Max}]\). The distribution is not uniform. SR_GameServer samples in several tiers: three samples take the min, combined with an extra random branch, then the distribution is shifted by the gap between the attacker’s Attack Rating and the target’s Parry Ratio.

  • The higher Attack Rating is above Parry Ratio, the closer the roll stays to Max.
  • The higher Parry Ratio is, the more the roll is pulled toward Min.
  • Electric Shock lowers Parry Ratio, so the target takes damage clustered near Max.

Attack Rating and Parry Ratio belong to this gate. They choose where \(A\) and \(B\) sit inside the range. Tier 3 receives the two rolled numbers and does not multiply by an extra parry factor. The calculator at the bottom also takes \(A\) and \(B\) as already rolled. The spec fixes the direction of the distribution; this page does not reproduce the internal parameters of each random sample.

Tier 3 — Two streams and the damage floor

Every hit runs both streams. A stream that is not part of the hit — a plain swing with no imbue turns the magic stream off — contributes DMG = 0. That is different from a stream that is present but fails to penetrate armor. A present stream with Raw ≤ 0 still deals 1.

\(C\), \(D\), and \(G\) are each the sum of sources of the same kind. They are added first, then placed in one spot in the formula. Gear +5% and Life Turnover +25% become \(G = 0.30\), so the factor \((1+G) = 1.30\).

\(L\) rises by about 3% per level when the attacker is higher than the target, and stops at +30%, so \(L \le 1.30\). The approximation used for estimates is \(L = \min(1.30,\; 1 + 0.03 \times \max(0, \mathrm{Lv}_{\mathrm{atk}} - \mathrm{Lv}_{\mathrm{def}}))\). The spec describes the case where the attacker is higher. When the attacker is lower, this estimate keeps \(L = 1\); it does not add a level penalty.

A skill database can store two coefficients: the main damage coefficient and a secondary coefficient for the imbue portion. Flame Wave uses 2.50 on the main stream and 0.83 on the added imbue portion. Each portion receives its own coefficient. Turn Rising in Test 1 has one coefficient, 2.08, on the whole \((A+B)\), so a pipeline with one \(H\) per stream applies directly. When the two coefficients differ, \(H\) is no longer a common factor that can be pulled out together with \(E\). The calculator keeps one \(H\) per stream, which matches the written formula and Test 1.

Three steps of each stream

Step 1, pass through absorption, then subtract armor:

\[ \mathrm{Raw}_{t}=\frac{(A_{t}+B_{t})\times(1+C_{t})}{1+D_{t}}-E_{t} \]

Step 2, amplify. \(F_{t}\) is the decimal already capped at 1.20. The STR/INT formulas in Tier 1 return that number directly, so it is not divided by 100 again. Divide by 100 only when the number in hand is a UI percent.

\[ \mathrm{Calc}_{t}=\mathrm{Raw}_{t}\times F_{t}\times(1+G_{t})\times H_{t}\times L \]

Step 3, apply the floor. This is the step that shortened formulas online drop, and those formulas then produce a negative. Raw ≤ 0 means the amount left after absorption does not exceed \(E_{t}\); the later factors must not turn a negative into damage. An active stream receives 1. An inactive stream receives 0, so a plain swing does not gain an extra point of magic.

\[ \begin{aligned} \mathrm{DMG}_{t}&=0 && \text{off}\\ \mathrm{DMG}_{t}&=1 && \mathrm{Raw}\le 0\\ \mathrm{DMG}_{t}&=\max\!\big(1,\lfloor\mathrm{Calc}_{t}\rfloor\big) && \mathrm{Raw}>0 \end{aligned} \]

With \(F\), \((1+G)\), \(H\), and \(L\) positive, the Raw ≤ 0 branch and the \(\max(1,\lfloor\mathrm{Calc}\rfloor)\) branch agree. The floor of 1 also catches hits whose Raw is positive but whose small \(H\) drops Calc below 1.

Tier 4 — Critical, multi-hit, Berserk

A normal hit, once each stream is an integer:

\[ \mathrm{Total}=\mathrm{DMG}_{\mathrm{phy}}+\mathrm{DMG}_{\mathrm{mag}} \]

A critical doubles exactly the floored physical DMG. The magic stream stays as it is:

\[ \mathrm{Total}_{\mathrm{crit}}=(2 \times \mathrm{DMG}_{\mathrm{phy}})+\mathrm{DMG}_{\mathrm{mag}} \]

A full-STR build keeps nearly all of its damage on the physical stream, so one critical lands near 200% of the normal hit. A full-INT build that is imbuing or nuking keeps most of its damage on the magic stream; the critical only lifts the physical part, and the increase in the total usually falls around 30%–50%, depending on the split between the two streams.

Multi-hit skills: each hit has its own \(H_{\mathrm{hit}}\) and runs Tier 2 through Tier 4 again. Three hits at 80% are three calculations with \(H = 0.80\), each with its own Block roll and its own Crit roll. Skill damage is the sum of the per-hit totals.

Berserk multiplies the output total of the hit. Original servers usually use \(\times 2\). Because \(Z\) is constant across hits, multiplying each hit and then adding equals adding the hits and then multiplying. A blocked hit is still 0 after Berserk.

Test 2 — Level 1 hits level 100

A normal hit, \(A_{\mathrm{phy}} = 20\), \(B = 0\), \(C = 0\), \(F_{\mathrm{phy}} = 0.95\). The target has \(D_{\mathrm{phy}} = 1.00\) and \(E_{\mathrm{phy}} = 3000\). With no imbue, the magic stream is off.

\[ \mathrm{Raw}_{\mathrm{phy}}=\frac{20 \times 1}{1+1.00}-3000=10-3000=-2990 \]

Raw is negative and the physical stream is on, so physical DMG = 1. Magic DMG = 0. Total = 1.

Add a level-1 imbue whose magic Raw also fails to beat armor, and the magic stream turns on and is forced to 1 as well. Total = 1 + 1 = 2. The spec for this case settles the floor branch; it does not list each magic variable. The calculator uses a boundary stand-in only so that magic Raw ≤ 0.

Scope

This spec follows the SR_GameServer architecture on the original Joymax / vSRO PC client. Dual-stream, balance after armor, absorption as \(1/(1+D)\), and a floor of 1 point are that frame. SROM Mobile and modified private servers may keep the frame and then attach a scale constant at the last step to tune PvP. This page does not put any scale constant into the formula.

This file is a calculation handbook for the pipeline that was checked. It does not replace a packet log, or the client number, when a server adds its own factor.
